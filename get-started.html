Set up Fytvio

A few quick steps to get your account and health data ready.

01

Create your account

Sign in and get your Fytvio account ready.

  1. Open Fytvio and choose a supported sign-in method.
  2. Confirm that you meet the 18+ age requirement.
  3. Review the health-data and AI-sharing choices presented during setup.
  4. Complete the profile details you want Fytvio to use.
02

Set up your profile

Choose the details and preferences Fytvio should use for you.

  1. Open Profile.
  2. Review your units, timezone, and other profile details.
  3. Add only the optional information you are comfortable providing.
  4. Save your changes.
03

Connect your health data

Let Fytvio use the health and fitness data you choose to share.

  1. Open the health-data connection option in Fytvio.
  2. On iPhone, authorize the Apple Health data types you want to share.
  3. On Android, authorize the Google Health Connect data types you want to share.
  4. Return to Fytvio and allow the first sync to complete.
You stay in control of the data categories you authorize, and manual logging remains available.
04

Make sure your data is showing up

Check that your recent health and fitness data made it into Fytvio.

  1. Open Today and My Data.
  2. Look for recent workouts, steps, sleep, or other authorized data.
  3. Check the displayed sync status when available.
  4. If a metric is missing, remember that not every source records every metric every day.
05

See what you should do today

Today brings your plan, readiness, and recent progress together in one place.

  1. Review your primary goal and current planned task.
  2. Check readiness and supporting signals when enough recent data is available.
  3. Open task details before starting a planned session.
  4. Review your Coach brief, recent numbers, and progress context.
06

Check how ready you are

Use readiness as a quick guide for how hard you may want to push today.

  1. Look at the score together with its supporting context.
  2. Use the guidance to help decide whether today is a day to push, stay steady, or take it easier.
  3. If Fytvio says data is limited, treat the score with less confidence.
  4. It’s a guide, not a command—how you actually feel still matters.
Plans & training

Goals, plans & workouts

Set a goal, follow your plan, and keep it realistic as life changes.

07

Create a goal

Tell Fytvio what you want to achieve and the timeframe you have in mind.

  1. Open the goal area.
  2. Choose the option to create a new goal.
  3. Describe the outcome clearly and add a target date when relevant.
  4. Look through the plan and make sure it feels realistic for you.
08

See what’s in your plan

Check your upcoming workouts, recovery days, and how the plan builds over time.

  1. Open the goal and expand its plan.
  2. Review the scheduled tasks, progression, and recovery days.
  3. Check that the plan fits your real calendar and other active goals.
  4. Change or skip anything that doesn’t fit your schedule or how you’re feeling.
09

Working on more than one goal?

Choose what matters most and keep your other goals from getting in the way.

  1. Review which goal is marked primary.
  2. Use secondary goals for additional objectives that should not replace the main focus.
  3. Check Today for overlapping tasks.
  4. If plans conflict, use Coach or plan adjustment to help simplify the week.
10

Start today’s workout

Open your planned session, do the workout, and save it when you’re done.

  1. Open the task from Today.
  2. Review the task details and start the session when appropriate.
  3. Use the session controls while you train.
  4. When finished, mark the session done and save it.
11

Add a workout yourself

Did a workout not sync? You can add it yourself.

  1. Open My Data and the Training area.
  2. Choose the workout/log action.
  3. Add the workout type and the details you know.
  4. Review the entry and save it.
12

Change your plan

Plans don’t have to be rigid. Update what’s ahead when your schedule or needs change.

  1. Open the goal you want to change.
  2. Choose the plan adjustment option.
  3. Select or describe what changed—schedule, difficulty, recovery, target date, or another supported reason.
  4. Review the proposed adjustment before accepting it.
Coach

Your Coach

Ask Coach about your goals, workouts, recovery, nutrition, or the patterns in your own data.

13

Ask Coach anything about your fitness

Ask naturally—just like you’d ask a coach who already knows your recent Fytvio data.

  1. Open Coach.
  2. Ask the question in your own words.
  3. Be specific when dates or goals matter—for example, “Compare my running this week with last week.”
  4. Use the answer as guidance and ask a follow-up if you want Coach to dig deeper.
14

Ask Coach what your numbers mean

Ask what changed, what looks different, or what you should pay attention to.

  1. Name the metric or period you care about.
  2. For comparisons, include the periods you want compared.
  3. For broad reviews, name the domains—training, sleep, HRV, nutrition, water, and so on.
  4. If there isn’t enough data to say something confidently, Coach should tell you.
Example: “Compare June, July, and August training and recovery. What changed most?”
15

Too much planned this week?

If several goals are piling up, ask Coach to help you decide what to prioritize.

  1. Ask for the specific period you want reviewed.
  2. Tell Coach which goal is your priority if it is not obvious.
  3. Review which existing sessions Coach recommends keeping, combining, moving, or deprioritizing.
  4. A Coach suggestion does not change your plan unless Fytvio asks you to confirm the change.
16

See how many Coach messages you have left

Check your included Coach usage whenever you want.

  1. Open your Profile or the Coach usage area.
  2. Review the monthly Coach allowance.
  3. Check the rolling 24-hour limit and remaining messages when shown.
  4. Tap the information icon for details about included and own-key usage when available.
My Data

Food, water & health data

Track the things that matter to you, whether they sync automatically or you add them yourself.

17

Add a meal

Tell Fytvio what you ate, check the nutrition details, and save it.

  1. Open My Data → Nutrition.
  2. Choose Add a meal.
  3. Enter what you ate; add brand or restaurant detail when useful.
  4. Review the returned calories and nutrients, edit anything needed, then save.
Published nutrition can be used for recognized branded items; other entries may be clearly labeled estimates.
18

Log your water

Keep a simple record of how much water you drink.

  1. Open the Nutrition area.
  2. Use the water logging control.
  3. Add the amount you drank in your selected units.
  4. Save the entry and repeat during the day as needed.
19

Set a reminder

Want a nudge for water or supplements? Set a reminder that works for you.

  1. Open the reminder area under Nutrition.
  2. Choose the reminder type.
  3. Select the time or schedule available in the app.
  4. Save the reminder and allow notifications if prompted by your device.
20

Keep track of supplements

Save the supplements you want to remember and make regular ones easier to log.

  1. Open Supplements in the Nutrition area.
  2. Add the supplement and the details you want to keep.
  3. Use favorites/quick actions when available for regular items.
  4. Use the end/stop option when you no longer want to track it going forward.
21

Add a health reading yourself

Add a reading manually when it isn’t coming from your connected health data.

  1. Open My Data and the Vitals area.
  2. Choose the relevant log action.
  3. Enter the measurement and review the units carefully.
  4. Save the entry.
22

See how you’re trending

Zoom out from today and see how your training, recovery, health, and nutrition are changing.

  1. Open Trends.
  2. Select the period you want to review.
  3. Move through Training, Recovery, Health, and Fuel sections where data is available.
  4. Pay attention to coverage and missing periods before drawing conclusions from a chart.
Privacy & account

Your privacy & account

Manage what you share, your plan, and your account.

23

Control what you share

Choose whether Fytvio can use your health data and whether it can be shared with AI features.

  1. Open your Profile/privacy settings.
  2. Choose whether Fytvio can collect the health data you allow.
  3. Choose separately whether that data can be used with AI features.
  4. You can change these choices later from your settings.
24

Manage access for a linked coach

See who you’ve linked and remove access whenever you want.

  1. Open the linked-coach/athlete sharing area.
  2. Check who you’re linking before you approve access.
  3. Linked coaches get read-only access to the Fytvio information shared through that connection.
  4. Remove the link whenever you no longer want to share access.
25

Manage your Fytvio plan

Check your current plan, compare options, or manage your subscription.

  1. Open the Plans/upgrade area from Fytvio.
  2. Compare the currently displayed plan features and allowances.
  3. Complete purchases through the supported app-store flow.
  4. Manage or cancel an existing Apple/Google subscription from the store account used to purchase it.
26

Delete your Fytvio account

If you decide to leave Fytvio, here’s how to permanently delete your account.

  1. Use the in-app Delete account control when available.
  2. If you cannot access the app, use the instructions on our account deletion page.
  3. Remember that deleting the app alone does not delete the account.
  4. Store subscriptions are managed separately through Apple or Google.
