Why Fytvio

Most fitness apps show you numbers. Fytvio helps you understand what they may mean.

A dashboard full of charts isn't a decision. Fytvio may combine signals such as sleep, HRV, steps, and training load into practical readiness guidance for the day, plus an AI coach that can help explain the context when you ask.

One number that actually means something

When sufficient data is available, your readiness score helps summarize the day: push, maintain, or take it easier. Fytvio also explains when available data is limited.

A real plan for your actual goal

Choose the level of structure that fits you: use AI-assisted planning, build your own schedule, or keep a simpler routine or time-since tracker. Fytvio keeps completed progress separate from what is still ahead.

Built for how training actually goes

Missed a sync, forgot to log a workout, or do not own a smartwatch? Fytvio works with the data you choose to provide and tells you when there is not enough information for confident guidance.

Nutrition, without the retyping

Log a meal by typing what you had. For supported branded or restaurant foods, Fytvio may use published nutrition information when available; other entries may use clearly labeled AI-assisted estimates. Nutrition data and estimates can be incomplete or inaccurate, so review them before relying on them. Track water and supplements, and save favorites for quicker repeat logging.

Personalized Goal Planning

Build a real plan around your goal—not a generic schedule.

Tell Fytvio what you want to achieve and when. It may use your current activity, recent workouts, recovery context, available health data, and stated constraints to generate a structured multi-week plan you can review and adjust as life changes.

How the planning engine works
1 · Your goal

Describe what you want

Examples:

  • Run my first 10K
  • Build to 100 push-ups
  • Walk 15,000 steps consistently
  • Improve strength over 8 weeks
→
2 · Fytvio understands

May check context and realism

  • Current fitness and recent activity
  • Typical workout distance or volume
  • Sleep, HRV, heart rate, and recovery where available
  • Health or injury context you choose to provide
  • Whether the goal is too broad, conflicting, or unrealistic
→
3 · Your plan

May generate a progressive schedule

  • Structured multi-week progression
  • Gradual workload increases
  • Recovery and lighter days
  • Clear daily tasks and targets
  • A final goal or assessment day

Typical static plan

  • Same schedule for everyone
  • Starts from a generic baseline
  • Ignores your recent activity
  • Little help when life gets in the way
  • May increase volume too quickly

Fytvio plan

  • Personalized from your available context
  • Keeps one primary goal clear while supporting additional plans
  • May apply progressive workload guardrails
  • Can be reviewed and adjusted easier or harder
  • Keeps completed progress while updating what remains

Plans may be generated or assisted by AI and may be incomplete, inaccurate, or unsuitable for a particular person. Review and adjust them before use. They are for fitness and wellness support, not medical treatment, injury rehabilitation, or professional supervision.

Linked Coaches & Trusted People

Share selected Fytvio information without handing over your whole account.

Where the feature is available, you may approve a coach or trusted person to view designated information. Fytvio does not employ, credential, recommend, endorse, or supervise linked people merely because they use the service.

You approve every connection

A coach only gains access after you accept their request. Revoke it anytime, and access stops immediately.

A coaching view, not a takeover

A linked coach sees your activity, readiness, and training progress — not your billing, private AI conversations, health-condition details, or account credentials.

You remain in control

Where supported, you may share designated training information and revoke future access. A linked person remains an independent user and is not endorsed or supervised by Fytvio.

Privacy, for real

You're trusting us with your health data. We don't take that lightly.

Health apps have a bad reputation for quietly monetizing exactly the data you didn't mean to share. Fytvio is built the other way: clear consent, no ad-driven business model, and controls that actually work when you use them.

We do not sell personal or health data

Fytvio does not sell, rent, or broker personal or consumer health data to advertisers or data brokers. See the Privacy Policy and Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy for details.

Default AI use is not for general model training

We do not authorize our default AI providers to use Fytvio prompts or health data to train their general models, subject to the provider terms and enterprise/API settings applicable to our use. If you use your own API key, your provider account settings and terms also apply.

Real export and deletion

Use available export controls, delete your account in the app, or request deletion through our website. After deletion, only narrowly limited pseudonymous compliance or legal records may remain as described in our Privacy Policy.

Consent you actually gave

Health-data collection and sharing with an AI provider are separate choices. You can withdraw either consent for future processing.
